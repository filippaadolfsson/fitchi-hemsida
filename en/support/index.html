Frequently asked questions

How do I connect my watch or health data?

On Android, Fitchi reads from Health Connect (for example Samsung Health or your watch). On iPhone, Fitchi reads from Apple Health. You allow it in the app the first time and can change the permission any time in Health Connect or under Settings → Health.

I’m getting a new phone. How do I bring my fitchi?

Turn on Backup under Your journey in the app and save your recovery code. On the new phone, tap “Already have a fitchi? Restore it” and enter the code.

How do I delete my data?

Tap Start over from scratch under Your journey. This deletes everything on the phone, as well as your friend profile and backup on our server. You can also email us. Read more in our privacy policy.

How do I turn off reminders?

Under Your journey → Reminders. You can also turn them off in your phone’s settings.

The app is in the wrong language.

Fitchi follows your phone’s language. You can switch between English and Swedish under Your journey → Språk · Language.

Is Fitchi a medical app?

No. Fitchi is an app for everyday habits and does not replace healthcare. Contact a healthcare provider if you’re worried about your health.
