Privacy policy

Fitchi is built so that most things stay on your phone. This page explains what does leave it, why, and how you stay in control.

Effective from 30 October 2026 · På svenska

In short

  • Your health data (steps, sleep, heart rate and workouts) is read on your phone and is not sent to us – unless you choose to turn on Backup.
  • You don’t need an account, an email address or a phone number.
  • If you use Friends, your fitchi’s name, character, mood and level are shared with the friends you add yourself.
  • We show no ads, don’t track you and never sell data.

Who is responsible?

The data controller is Flipp Marketing AB (reg. no. 559426-3484), Sweden. Contact us at hej@fitchi.app if you have questions or want to exercise your rights.

What is only stored on your phone

The following is stored in the app on your phone (or in your browser if you use the web app) and is not sent to us, unless you turn on Backup yourself (see below):

  • Your answers in the matching quiz and the fitchi you got.
  • The game: level, coins, keys, items, quests and your diary.
  • Workouts, sleep, naps and water that you log yourself.
  • Health data from Apple Health or Health Connect (see the next section).

Reminders are created on the phone. If you delete the app, or tap Start over from scratch under Your journey, everything is deleted.

Health data, Apple Health and Health Connect

If you allow it, the iPhone app can read steps, sleep, heart rate, resting heart rate and workouts from Apple Health, and the Android app can read the same from Health Connect (for example from Samsung Health or your watch). Fitchi does not write any data to Apple Health or Health Connect.

The data is only used to show it to you and to work out how your fitchi is feeling: energy, calm and strength, which quests are complete, and the week’s keys. The health data itself is stored on the phone and is never shared with anyone else. It only leaves the phone if you turn on Backup yourself, and then only so that you can get it back.

Health data is never used for advertising, never sold, never shared with third parties and never used to assess creditworthiness or insurance. On iPhone, Fitchi follows Apple’s rules for HealthKit. On Android, Fitchi’s use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can remove access at any time: on iPhone under Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Fitchi, and on Android in Health Connect.

Fitchi is an app for everyday habits, not a medical service, and does not make diagnoses.

Friends

Only you and the friends you have added yourself can see this data. The legal basis is contract: you are using a feature you have chosen. The data is kept for as long as you use Friends. If you tap Start over from scratch, your profile, friendships and cheers are deleted from the server.

The server is run by Supabase and is located in the EU (Ireland). Supabase processes the data on our behalf under a data processing agreement.

Backup

Backup is optional and switched off by default. If you turn it on under Your journey, a packed copy of everything that is otherwise only on your phone is stored on our server: the game, your answers in the matching quiz, workouts, sleep and water you have logged, how you have said you feel, and the health data the app has saved (steps, sleep and heart rate). Its only purpose is to let you get your fitchi back if you change or lose your phone.

The copy is linked to an anonymous account without a name, email address or phone number. You get a recovery code that only you have; we only store a hash of the code and cannot read the code itself. The copy is not used for anything else, is not shared with anyone and is not shown to your friends.

Because the copy may contain health data, the legal basis is your explicit consent, which you give when you turn the feature on. You can withdraw your consent at any time by turning Backup off – the copy is then deleted from the server immediately. It is also deleted if you tap Start over from scratch. The copy is stored encrypted with Supabase in the EU (Ireland), on the same server as Friends.

The website and the web app

The website and the web app are hosted on GitHub Pages, which technically needs your IP address to show the pages. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which also sees your IP address. We use no tracking cookies and no analytics or advertising services.

Children

Fitchi is not intended for children under 13.

Your rights

You have the right to know what data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to the processing. Most of it is only on your phone and is controlled by you. For Friends and Backup you can delete everything yourself in the app, or email us with your friend code. You can also lodge a complaint with the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (imy.se) or the data protection authority where you live.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top. We will tell you about bigger changes in the app.
